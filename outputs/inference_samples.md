## 1. What is the procedure for escalating a triage queue delay at the Kisumu facility?

The threshold is 30 minutes past the allocated triage slot. At that point the staff member logs the case under Queue Escalation, tags the duty nurse practitioner, and notes the patient's status in the log. The escalation board is reviewed every 15 minutes, so a properly logged case is picked up quickly. As with every workflow, clinical decisions stay with the AfyaPlus clinical team.

## 2. A patient missed their appointment last week. How do they reschedule?

Go to My Appointments, select the missed visit, and choose Reschedule. The window is 14 days from the missed date; after that the system closes the booking and the patient needs a new referral code, which only the reviewing clinician can issue. Front-desk staff cannot override an expired booking. For anything that needs clinical judgement, direct the patient to an AfyaPlus provider.

## 3. A staff member is locked out of the staff portal. What are the steps?

Verify the staff member's identity against the duty roster, then raise a lockout ticket in the admin console. Only the facility administrator can reset credentials. Sharing another person's login as a workaround is a violation of the AfyaPlus access policy and must never happen, even under time pressure. Clinical questions are always routed to an AfyaPlus provider rather than answered at the desk.

If a patient sees Pending review, that means a clinician is handling the release personally and will contact them, so no action is needed from the front desk beyond confirming the contact number is current. The duty administrator handles all these steps, so hand the documents over and let them complete the registration. ## 7. The dispensary has run out of an item on a patient's prescription. What do we tell the patient?

The expected restock date on the item card is the information to share with the patient. Any change to what was prescribed is for the prescriber to decide, not dispensary or front-desk staff.
